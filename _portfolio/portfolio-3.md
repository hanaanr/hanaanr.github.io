Researched LLM infrastructure for the Agora Citizen Network, a privacy-conscious, open-source platform for depolarizing political discussions. 

Evaluated different LLMs to understand cost-performance tradeoffs and use-case suitability, and engineered prompts to generate clear summaries, topic labels, and group descriptors - helping participants understand where people agree, disagree, and why. These improvements aimed to make large-scale public dialogue more transparent and accessible.
